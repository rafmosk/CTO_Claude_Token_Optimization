# Code of Conduct

This project welcomes everyone who wants to use Claude more efficiently, whatever their background or experience.

## Expected

- Be respectful and assume good intent.
- Criticize ideas and measurements, never people.
- Back claims with evidence, and accept being asked for it.
- Help newcomers: a beginner's question is a contribution too.

## Not accepted

- Insults, harassment, discrimination or personal attacks
- Sharing other people's personal data
- Spam, self-promotion unrelated to the project, or selling services
- Posting techniques meant to bypass usage limits, terms of service or safety measures

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove contributions and block users who do not respect these rules. Report problems to a maintainer through GitHub.
